Why pest control in California is different

Drywood termites are a major problem in Southern California and coastal areas, living inside framing, furniture and attics without any soil contact. That is why whole-house fumigation under colorful tents is common in Los Angeles, San Diego and Orange County. Western subterranean termites are active statewide, and Formosan termites have been found in parts of San Diego County.

Argentine ants form huge interconnected colonies across coastal and inland California and invade kitchens in waves, especially in hot, dry weather. Roof rats travel along palm trees, ivy and utility lines into attics in Los Angeles, the Bay Area and Sacramento. Black widows, earwigs and silverfish thrive in the Central Valley's hot summers, and mosquitoes that carry West Nile virus are tracked each year by local vector control districts.

Pest control tips for California residents

  • Trim ivy, palms and trees away from the roof to cut off roof rat access.
  • Keep a written record of termite fumigations and warranties for future buyers.
  • Use bait stations instead of sprays for Argentine ants, which can split colonies when stressed.

Pest control licensing in California

Structural pest control in California is licensed by the Structural Pest Control Board, part of the Department of Consumer Affairs, which issues licenses for fumigation (Branch 1), general pest (Branch 2) and wood-destroying organisms (Branch 3). Wood-destroying organism inspection reports are filed with the board, and fumigation has strict notice and safety rules. Verify a license on the board's website.

Pest control in California: questions

Why are homes tented for termites in California?

Tenting, or structural fumigation, is used when drywood termites are spread through a home's framing. The fumigant gas reaches termites inside the wood that spot treatments can't.

Do you cover all of California?

Yes. PestHalt connects callers in all 304 California cities and communities over 25,000 people, including Los Angeles, San Diego, San Jose, San Francisco, Fresno, Sacramento, and in smaller towns and rural areas served by local providers.

Who licenses pest control companies in California?

Pest control in California is licensed by the California Structural Pest Control Board (Department of Consumer Affairs). Ask any provider for its license number and verify it with the agency before work begins.
