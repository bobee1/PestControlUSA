Why pest control in Nebraska is different

Mice move into Nebraska homes in large numbers after harvest and the first cold nights. Eastern subterranean termites are active in eastern Nebraska, including the Omaha and Lincoln metros.

Southeastern Nebraska lies at the edge of the brown recluse range, and recluses are found in some Omaha and Lincoln homes. Boxelder bugs and lady beetles gather on homes in fall, and ticks and mosquitoes are common along the Platte and Missouri rivers.

Nebraska's population is concentrated in Omaha and Lincoln, with older brick bungalows and two-story homes with basements that termites and mice can exploit, while Grand Island, Kearney and North Platte are surrounded by irrigated cropland. Grain storage, farm shops and acreage homes along the Platte River all draw rodents, and the river corridor's cottonwood stands are favorite overwintering spots for boxelder bugs.

Pest control tips for Nebraska residents

  • Trim boxelder trees near the house or seal the sunny walls bugs gather on.
  • Seal gaps around garage doors and foundation vents before October.
  • Check basements for termite tubes on block walls each spring.
